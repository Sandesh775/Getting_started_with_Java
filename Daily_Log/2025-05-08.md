### 1. Word Frequency Analyzer
- **File:** "Everyday exercises/OOPs basic/Class and Object/Practice 7/Word_Frequency_Analyzer.java"
- **Concept:** Manual frequency counting using parallel arrays
- **Time:** O(n²)
- **Space:** O(n)
- **Key Learning:** Parallel arrays tracking (unique[] and count[])
- **Mistake:** Incremented uniqueSize on duplicate words → fixed by incrementing only when adding new word
- **Mistake:** Printed unique.length instead of uniqueSize → fixed loop range
- **Key Insight:** unique[i] and count[i] share same index to relate word with its frequency
